What is Stub?

Turn event screenshots into calendar entries — no typing, no copy-paste.

You see an event on Instagram, in an email, or on a poster. Instead of manually creating a calendar entry, drop the screenshot into Stub. It reads the image, pulls out the details, and lets you add the event to Google Calendar in one tap.

How it works

  1. Upload or paste a screenshot of any event — invite, poster, story, email, ticket.
  2. Gemini reads the image and fills in a ticket stub you can edit before saving.
  3. Sign in with Google and add the event to your calendar.

Your data stays yours

Stub runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

  • Your Gemini API key is stored only in this browser's local storage.
  • Screenshots go directly from your device to Google's Gemini API.
  • Google sign-in uses OAuth — Stub never sees your Google password.

What you need

  • A free Gemini API key — paste it in Settings (⚙) on the app page.
  • A Google account — to add events to your calendar.

Always double-check the extracted ticket before adding. If a date or time isn't clear in the screenshot, Stub may leave it blank rather than guess wrong.
